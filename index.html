It is not a rigid plan. One or two things to draw on, then room to stop because the view is good, or because something smells right. The wandering is part of the day.

What helps

Write like you are briefing a friend who will pick spots for you. The day is built from this note, the date, the weather, and the budget — not a fixed list.

  • Food: coffee, tacos, pasta, vegetarian, gelato, a nicer dinner, no seafood.
  • Pace: easy walking, people watching, dig through shops, live music if it is on.
  • What has worked before, and what to skip.
  • Driving: say if you need parking, or if you are already downtown on foot.
