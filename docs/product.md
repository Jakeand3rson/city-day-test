# Product (proposed)

## The idea in one line

Drop people in the right pocket of a city so they stumble onto things while walking.

That's the value. Not a schedule. Not a list of 40 places. A good starting area, a mood, directions, and room to wander.

It works for a date. It works for seeing a new city. Same shape either way.

## Web page only

No app. The page we already made proved a link is enough. People open it on their phone, tap directions, and go. Nothing to install.

## Free

Like the page that already exists.

- One city.
- A few moods to pick from.
- Directions to get you into an area.
- No account. You can try it before signing in to anything.
- Good for only a few days.
- **Choices reset when you reopen the page.** That's on purpose. Reset is the free product, not a bug.
- **Not a saved, shared plan.** Anyone can open the free page, but it doesn't remember your day, so there's nothing saved to share.
- **No end-of-day report.** Free doesn't remember the day, so there's nothing to recap.

The free page's job is to get you into an area. That's it.

## Paid: $15 per trip (proposed)

A "trip" means one day. It is not a subscription.

What you get:

- **The link keeps your choices.** It does not reset when you reopen it.
- **A short personal brief** (below), not a wall of settings.
- **Light edits after that.** Swap a stop. Shift dinner. That kind of thing.
- **Sharing with the people on the day.** A couple of other people can open your paid day. It's for the people you're actually out with, not a public link anyone can pass around.
- **An end-of-day report.** After the day, a short recap of where you went. Meant to be fun to look back on. This is part of paid now, not a maybe-later.
- **Same wandering shape** as free. The paid day is more personal, not more rigid.

### The paid brief (still a short list)

1. City
2. Date
3. Who it's for
4. A dollar amount for the day, checked against parking, food, and a ticketed stop like a museum
5. What you like to eat
6. Drinking: yes, no, or doesn't matter. This changes the agenda. We don't assume either way.
7. Pace
8. Style: **mystery adventure** (vague moods that reveal as you go, like the Saturday page) or a **plain wandering agenda** you can see up front.
9. Must-sees or hidden gems: the city's well-known spots, or things locals post about and things we find.
10. One thing the day must include

That's it. One screen of questions, not a catalog of toggles.

### Activities

A paid day can include things happening that day (events) and unique places: a mural, a climbing wall locals know, that kind of thing. Question 9 decides whether we lean toward must-sees or hidden gems. There's no separate activity menu.

### Sign-in (needed for paid)

To pay, you sign in with an account you already have (Google or similar). We do not store passwords.

Why: a paid day, the people it's shared with, and the end-of-day report need to belong to someone. Sign-in is how we know whose day it is.

Free stays open. You can try the free page without signing in.

## Safety (proposed)

We'll use crime and safety information when choosing which areas to send people toward. It shapes where the day goes.

We will not label neighborhoods "sketchy" in the product, and we will not publish a crime callout. Safety is folded into the choices, not shown as warnings.

## What paid refuses to customize

We're saying no to these on purpose, so the product stays simple:

- No minute-by-minute itinerary.
- No long settings page or quiz beyond the brief above.
- No rebuilding the whole day over and over. Light edits only.
- No public link dump. Sharing is for the few people on the day.
- No passwords we store. Sign-in only through an existing account.
- No app.

If someone wants a rigid itinerary, this isn't it. That's fine.

## Where we start: one city

St. Petersburg / Sarasota. That day is the proof, so that's where we start. Other cities come later, only if this one works. When and which: unknown.

## Who it's for

- A couple who wants a good day out without planning it.
- A small group out together who all want the plan on their own phone.
- Someone visiting a city for the first time who wants to land in the right area and explore on foot.
- People who like wandering more than checklists.

Who it's probably not for: people who want every hour booked, or people who want a full travel agent.

## Future (not in phases 1 to 3)

Ideas we like but are not planning or gating on yet:

- **Scavenger-hunt versions** of a day.
- **Groups that split up** and still meet at a set time for one shared thing, like a meal.
- **Booked experiences**, including paid tours and car rentals. Much later. The current day is a nudge: a few open stops, then room to wander.

These are not part of any phase and not part of what $15 buys today.
