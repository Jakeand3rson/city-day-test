# Who pays (proposed)

Only three kinds of people are likely to pay $15 for one day. They're ranked below. Marketing and the first build aim at the first one. The other two are real, but we are not splitting this into three products. Same brief, different defaults, different ads.

No market sizes here. We don't have real numbers, so we're not making any up.

## 1. The occasion (first, and who we build for)

A couple with one scarce day. An anniversary, a birthday, the first day without the kids, a Saturday that has to feel made.

**Why they pay:** the day won't come again. A link they can hand someone is the gift.

**What they want:** mystery more often than a list.

**What they need from the plan:**

- Sharing with the other person
- The end-of-day report, as a keepsake
- A rough budget
- Drinking as a real question: yes, no, or doesn't matter. We don't assume.
- One thing the day must include

**Marketing line:** send them a link. Not "plan your vacation."

This is who the Saturday page already was. The free page and the first sharing should feel like this.

## 2. The one-day visitor (second, the wider group)

In town for a wedding, a conference, family, or a weekend. One free day in a city they don't know.

**Why they pay:** they're already spending far more than $15 on food. Next to wasting the day, the price is cheap.

**What they want more often:**

- The plain agenda, visible up front
- Must-sees
- Events happening that day
- A budget
- Drinking (yes, no, or doesn't matter)
- The report, as how they remember the city

This is the broader audience if we ever run ads. We don't aim the first build only at them, because a vague page disappoints them faster.

## 3. The host (third, smaller)

Lives near the city. Friends or parents are in town until Sunday.

**Why they pay:** they want a plan they can share.

**What they want:**

- Hidden gems, not the obvious pier
- Sharing with the guest
- The plain agenda, so they can steer
- A budget, so dinner isn't embarrassing

This group is smaller than the other two. Locals with nobody visiting mostly won't pay. We're saying that plainly.

## Who we don't target

- **People planning a full week.** They want a map with 40 pins. That's not this.
- **Backpackers hunting free.** The free page is there, but we don't build for them.
- **Locals with no guest.** See above.
- **Big groups and bar crawls.** Maybe later, not now.

## What each one changes in the product

Only features already in the plan. No new phases, no new price, no new settings screens. Same brief for everyone. What changes is what we show first and what the ads say.

| Who | Defaults and emphasis |
|---|---|
| The occasion | Mystery as the style we show first, sharing, the report, drinking, budget, one must |
| The one-day visitor | Plain agenda available up front, must-sees, events that day, budget, drinking, the report |
| The host | Hidden gems, sharing with the guest, plain agenda, budget |
