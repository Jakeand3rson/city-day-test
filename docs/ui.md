# UI notes (proposed)

## Where the style comes from

The style source is [littlemousestudios.com](http://littlemousestudios.com/), Emily Anderson's portfolio.

What the live site actually is: a quiet Squarespace portfolio. The page itself is white and near-black. #272727, #222, and #000 show up heavily in the site's CSS. Type on the site includes Helvetica Neue, Proxima Nova, Brandon Grotesque, and Futura PT. The color lives in the project images, not painted across the page.

**The lesson:** the page stays quiet so the day can be the color.

- One idea at a time.
- Big titles.
- Lots of air.
- Phone first.
- No app-style tab bar.
- No map as the home screen.

## Colors

Accents are rare. Red is for the one action on a screen, not for errors everywhere.

## Fonts

A geometric sans in the same family as the portfolio's type.

We will not ship Proxima Nova, Brandon Grotesque, or Futura unless we license them. Whether we have or need a license for any of them: unknown, and we won't pretend otherwise. A system font stack or an open geometric sans is fine.

## Screens (few)

**First screen.** One line and one action, like a project title on the portfolio. For a mystery day, don't show the places yet.

**Paid brief.** One screen with the questions already listed in [product.md](product.md). Not a wizard with 10 steps.

**During the day.** A mood or a stop, a directions button, and room to wander. Not a timeline.

**The report.** Reads like a short printed recap of where you went. Not a dashboard. No charts.

## What we refuse

- Stock travel photos as the brand.
- A homepage that's a map full of pins.
- Neon on every button.
- More than one primary button on a screen.
