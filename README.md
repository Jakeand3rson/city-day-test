# City Day, friends and family

A free test of making a day in St. Petersburg. No payment and no account.

Open the page, name the day, and share the link. Creators get the form and a desk to rebuild. Recipients open a `#play/…` link and get the walk — moods and reveals only, not the form.

https://jakeand3rson.github.io/city-day-test/

Local check: `npm i && node qa-verify.mjs`
