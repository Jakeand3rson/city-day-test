# City Day, friends and family

A free test of the paid day in St. Petersburg. No payment and no account.

The creator fills in a short brief and gets a desk with a share link. The people going open a `#play/…` link and play the day the way the original anniversary page worked:

- Pick what's calling, reveal one place, go, tap "We're here," and pick again.
- One fixed point for the day (a dinner, or something you already booked), with a leave-by time.
- "Show me the list" any time, louder once the mystery goes stale.
- "Sky looks iffy" for an indoor plan B, "Vibes are cooling" to wind down.
- "Our day so far": every stop you tapped and everything you found off the list, with loved it / not for us.

The creator can turn on "Surprise us too" to play their own day without seeing the places.

https://jakeand3rson.github.io/city-day-test/

Local check: `npm install && npm test`

Decisions and direction live in the planning repo, `city-day` (`docs/reviews/2026-10-direction.md`).
